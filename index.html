On Demand FAQs

Are the classes suitable for beginners?

Yes. Our classes are designed for all fitness levels, including beginners and older adults who want safe and enjoyable movement programs.

Can I watch classes anytime?

Yes. On Demand classes are available so you can watch and move whenever it works best for your schedule.

Do I need any special equipment?

Most classes can be done with simple space to move. If a class uses props or equipment, it will be mentioned before you begin.

What types of classes do you offer?

Fitness Arts includes yoga, Pilates, dance, Tai Chi, creative movement, and active aging programs.

Are the dance classes beginner-friendly?

Yes. The dance classes are designed to feel approachable, expressive, and enjoyable for beginners.
